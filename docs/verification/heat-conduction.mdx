# Heat conduction

Verification of [`heatConductionScript`](../models/heat-conduction) against published benchmarks and closed-form
solutions. All results are from FEAScript-core 0.4.0 (RC), solved with `lusolve`.

The benchmark tests run on one mesh each. The convergence studies below were run for these pages with the same
setups on a sequence of structured meshes, refined by a factor of 2 each time.

## NAFEMS T4

A 0.6 m × 1.0 m plate, $k = 52$ W/(m·K), with a fixed temperature of 100 °C on the bottom edge, an insulated
left edge, and convection ($h = 750$ W/(m²·K), $T_\infty = 0$ °C) on the top and right edges. The target is the
temperature at point E (0.6 m, 0.2 m): **18.3 °C** in the NAFEMS standard benchmarks (TNSB Rev. 3, 1990), the
value used by most codes. COMSOL quotes 18.25 °C, citing the 1986 NAFEMS summary of the thermal benchmarks
(Cameron, Casey and Simpson).

![NAFEMS T4 plate with its boundary conditions and point E](/assets/verification/nafems-t4-geometry.svg)

![12 × 20 quadratic mesh of the NAFEMS T4 test](/assets/verification/nafems-t4-mesh.svg)

| Mesh    | Linear (°C) | Quadratic (°C) |
| ------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| 3 × 5   | 8.4996      | 21.5227        |
| 6 × 10  | 17.9540     | 18.3984        |
| 12 × 20 | 18.0927     | **18.2558**    |
| 24 × 40 | 18.2137     | 18.2539        |
| 48 × 80 | 18.2438     | 18.2538        |

Both element orders converge to 18.254 °C, which agrees with 18.25 °C and rounds to 18.3 °C. The plot shows the
error against the finest quadratic result (18.2538 °C). Linear elements
converge at second order on the finer meshes, quadratic elements at fourth order or faster.

## Code_Aster TPLP302

From the Code_Aster validation manual
([V4.05.302](https://ericca.uqtr.ca/fr12.4/man_v/v4/v4.05.302.pdf)): a strip 0.2 m wide and 2 m long, $k = 1$,
with 100 °C on the bottom edge, 0 °C on both side edges and an insulated top edge. The strip is ten widths long,
so the solution is that of a semi-infinite strip (Welty, Wicks and Wilson):

$$
T(x, y) = \frac{4 T_p}{\pi} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2n+1} \, e^{-(2n+1)\pi y / l} \,
\sin\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{l},
$$

with $T_p = 100$ °C and $l = 0.2$ m. The temperature jumps from 100 °C to 0 °C at the two bottom corners.

## Code_Aster TPLP01

From the Code_Aster validation manual ([V4.05.001](https://ericca.uqtr.ca/fr12.4/man_v/v4/v4.05.001.pdf)),
which takes the problem and its reference values from the VPCS validation guide (SFM/AFNOR, 1990, after Symm,
NPL Report NAC 31, 1973). An L-shaped domain, a 0.8 m × 0.8 m square without its 0.4 m × 0.4 m bottom right
quarter, $k = 1$, with 10 °C on the left edge, 0 °C on the right edge ($x = 0.8$, $0.4 \le y \le 0.8$) and all
other edges insulated. The temperature gradient is singular at the re-entrant corner (0.4, 0.4).

![Code_Aster TPLP01 L-shaped domain with its boundary conditions, the re-entrant corner C and the 13 reference points](/assets/verification/tplp01-geometry.svg)

The meshes are made with Gmsh, with an element size of 0.05 m refined to 0.01 m at the corner: 618 six-node
triangles and 321 nine-node quadrilaterals, both with a node at each reference point.

Triangles and quadrilaterals agree within 0.001 °C, and the values hardly change on a mesh of about 20,000 nodes.
The largest deviation from the reference is 0.24 %, apart from the point marked ¹.

¹ At (0.2, 0.2) the published 9.001 °C is 1.1 % below the FEAScript result, which is mesh-converged
(9.1007 °C on the 20,000-node mesh). It is likely a misprint of about 9.10 °C.

The test checks every point on both meshes with a relative tolerance of `5e-3` (`1.5e-2` at the point
marked ¹):
[`heatConduction2DCodeAsterTplp01`](https://github.com/FEAScript/FEAScript-core/tree/main/tests/verification/benchmark/heatConduction2DCodeAsterTplp01).
This is an independent reproduction and is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with EDF, the Code_Aster
project, SFM or AFNOR.

## Spatially varying coefficients

Closed-form solutions with spatially varying $k$ and $Q$, chosen so that the finite element solution is exact at
the nodes. Every nodal value must match to within `1e-10`; the observed deviations are of order `1e-15`.

![Domains and meshes of the spatially varying coefficient tests: 8 elements in 1D, 4 × 3 quadratic elements in 2D](/assets/verification/varying-coefficients.svg)

| Dimension | $k$     | $Q$ | Exact solution      | Elements          |
| --------- | ------- | --- | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| 1D        | 1       | 1   | $T = x (1 - x) / 2$ | Linear            |
| 1D        | $1 + x$ | −1  | $T = x$             | Linear, quadratic |
| 2D        | $1 + x$ | −1  | $T = x$             | Quadratic quads   |
| 2D        | $1 + y$ | −1  | $T = y$             | Quadratic quads   |

The 1D test also checks the frontal solver against `lusolve`. Tests:
[`heatConduction1DVaryingCoefficients`](https://github.com/FEAScript/FEAScript-core/tree/main/tests/verification/analytical/heatConduction1DVaryingCoefficients)
and
[`heatConduction2DVaryingCoefficients`](https://github.com/FEAScript/FEAScript-core/tree/main/tests/verification/analytical/heatConduction2DVaryingCoefficients).
